Positioning ▲ 180% inbound

From feature lists to outcome narratives

Situation
Core institutional data products were being sold on specs and features, which made them hard to tell apart for buyers.
Move
Repositioned around outcomes buyers care about — integration speed and workflow fit — and rebuilt messaging architecture and buyer journeys for high-intent segments.
Result
180% YoY increase in inbound leads and direct support for multiple high-value enterprise contract wins.

The Scaled Growth model

Situation
House accounts representing 15% of total business revenue needed consistent coverage, and leads were decaying between message and hand-off.
Move
Architected one model linking message creation directly to pipeline hand-off: digital-first campaigns, trigger-based account engagement and automated SDR tooling, run by a team of three.
Result
A single system from narrative to pipeline, with Salesforce reporting on pipeline health and campaign ROI.

  • Piper AI
  • Salesforce
  • Team lead
